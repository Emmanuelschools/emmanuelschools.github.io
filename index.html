About our school

Emmanuel Nursery and Primary School is a caring school for children from creche to primary 5. We give every child a strong start in reading, writing, numbers and good character, in a friendly and safe environment where parents and teachers work together.

Our classes

Creche

Gentle, loving care for our youngest children, with songs, play and early learning.

Nursery

Early reading, counting, drawing and play that prepare children for primary school.

Primary 1 to 5

A solid foundation in English, mathematics, science, social studies and moral values.
